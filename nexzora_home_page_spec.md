# NEXZORA - Phase 1: Home Page Specification

## 1. Global Header (Sticky / Frozen Pane)
*   **Behavior:** Fixed at the top of the viewport. Must feature a clean white background that gains a subtle, refined shadow or blur effect upon scrolling.
*   **Left Component:** NEXZORA Logo + Wordmark (Rendered sharply).
*   **Center Navigation:** 
    *   Products | Services | Industries | Innovation | Company | Careers
    *   *Interaction:* Each navigation item should support a highly polished mega-menu/drop-down structure (use clean stubs for phase 1).
*   **Right Component:** Prominent "Contact Us" CTA button. 
    *   *Styling:* Solid NEXZORA Orange or Navy Blue. Clean padding, premium typography.
    *   *Action:* Triggers a smooth scroll to the footer or opens a dedicated contact modal.

## 2. Hero Section (Introduction)
*   **Layout:** Minimum 75vh (3/4 screen height). Two-column split layout for strong visual hierarchy.
*   **Left Column (Messaging):**
    *   Pre-heading: NEXZORA TECHNOLOGIES
    *   Main Headline/Quote: *"The Next Zenith of Innovation — Engineering a New Era of Technology"* (Stylized to be large, impactful, and easily readable).
    *   CTA Buttons: [Explore Our Solutions] (Primary Solid) | [Discover NEXZORA] (Secondary Outline/Ghost).
*   **Right Column (Visuals):**
    *   *Feature:* Elegant animated interactive capability ribbons. 
    *   *Execution:* A code-driven (canvas or advanced Framer Motion) geometric ribbon effect utilizing the brand's navy and orange gradients. It should react subtly to mouse movement to feel alive, completely avoiding static stock photography or generic 3D blobs.

## 3. Engineering Capabilities Section
*   **Layout Concept:** Strictly avoid walls of text. Implement a clean, horizontal timeline or tab-based line interface.
*   **Capabilities List:**
    1. Embedded Engineering
    2. Automotive & Mobility
    3. Semiconductor & Chip Engineering
    4. AI & Robotics
    5. Cybersecurity
    6. Engineering Consulting
*   **Interaction:** 
    *   Display the capabilities in a sleek horizontal row (visualized as connected nodes or a progress track).
    *   *Action:* Clicking a capability seamlessly expands an individual section/card directly below the horizontal line, utilizing a smooth height transition to reveal placeholder content without jarring page jumps.
