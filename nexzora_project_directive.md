# NEXZORA - Project Directives

## 1. Brand Identity
*   **Brand Name:** NEXZORA TECHNOLOGIES
*   **Tagline:** "The Next Zenith of Innovation — Engineering a New Era of Technology"
*   **Primary Colors (Extracted from Logo):** 
    *   **Deep Corporate Navy:** `#041E42` or `#0A2540` (Represents trust, depth, engineering precision)
    *   **Vibrant Zenith Orange:** `#FF5A00` to `#E65C00` (Represents innovation, energy, forward motion)
*   **Backgrounds:** Crisp White (`#FFFFFF`) for the main canvas, with subtle off-whites/light grays (`#F8F9FA`) to establish depth.
*   **Typography:** Modern, geometric sans-serif (e.g., *Inter*, *Space Grotesk*, or *Montserrat*). Headings should utilize slight letter-spacing (tracking) to convey a premium, expansive feel.
*   **Visual Language:** Clean structural lines and sharp geometric accents mirroring the 'N' logo mark. Drop shadows should be highly diffuse and subtle. Avoid generic blob shapes or overly playful rounded corners.

## 2. UI/UX Principles (Avoiding the "Generic AI" Look)
*   **Space & Structure:** Layouts must be minimal, breathable, and highly structured. Avoid centering everything; use strong left-alignments for text to convey an editorial, professional engineering aesthetic.
*   **Animations (Framer Motion):** Animations must be physics-based (springs, slight damping) rather than linear fades. The interactions should feel tactile, snappy, and fluid.
*   **Micro-interactions:** Buttons should feature subtle scale or border-color shifts. Navigations and menus should fade and slide down gracefully without bouncing.

## 3. Tech Stack Context
*   Targeting React / Next.js architecture.
*   Tailwind CSS for utility-driven styling.
*   Framer Motion for complex interactive elements (ribbons, capability expanding lines).
